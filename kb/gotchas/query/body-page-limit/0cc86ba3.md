# knomit_query returns ~400-character snippets; include_body returns full bodies at most 5 per page, so read one fact in full with knomit_explain

- A `knomit_query` result row carries the body cut to a snippet of about 400 characters, with `body_truncated: true`, when the body is longer than that. A body of 400 characters or fewer comes back whole, without `body_truncated`. Deciding from a snippet misses whatever the fact says after it.
- `include_body: true` returns full bodies, with a page size of 3 by default and at most 5. Without it, pages are 20 by default, at most 100.
- More results come with a `cursor`: pass it back (with no other filters; they are ignored) for the next page.
- For one fact's full body, its history and its refs, call `knomit_explain` with the `file` value from the result row.

```json knomit_explain
{"binding": "<handle>", "file": "kb/howto/…/<id>.md"}
```
