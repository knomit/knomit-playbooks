# Declare context keys on a topic with a context: block (string, number, bool, enum, time); a fact under the topic may carry only declared keys, and knomit_query filters by them

`context` is a map of short typed labels on a fact that let it be found by a property that is not its subject, such as the task it belongs to or a verdict.

**Declare the keys** on a topic node of `.knomit/ontology.yaml` (from the mission template):

```yaml
topics:
  annotations:
    context:
      kind:       {type: enum, values: [verdict], required: true}
      verdict:    {type: enum, values: [corroborate, contradict]}
      confidence: {type: number, min: 0, max: 1}
```

- Types: `string`, `number`, `bool`, `enum` (needs `values`), `time`.
- Options: `required`; `pattern` (a Go regexp, unanchored, string only); `min` and `max` (inclusive, number only); `max_len` (string only, at most 256 bytes).
- A key is declared for a fact when the `context:` block of its topic or of any ancestor names it; a child may redeclare a key, and the nearest declaration wins. There is no root-level block. A topic with no block on its walk accepts no context.

**Write it** with the fact:

```json knomit_learn
{"binding": "<handle>", "moment_name": "verdicts",
 "facts": [{"topic": "annotations", "category": "t-17", "title": "…", "body": "…",
            "context": {"kind": "verdict", "verdict": "contradict", "confidence": 0.3}}]}
```

An undeclared key, a wrong type, a value outside its enum or range, or a missing required key refuses the whole call and names the key. Limits: keys match `[a-z][a-z0-9_]*`, at most 32 characters, at most 16 keys per fact; values are strings, numbers or booleans only; a string is one line, at most 128 bytes unless `max_len` allows up to 256.

**Find by it**: `knomit_query` with `context`, combined with any other filter, `path` included:

```json knomit_query
{"binding": "<handle>", "path": "kb/annotations/t-17/", "context": {"kind": "verdict"}}
```

knomit never acts on a context value: it is data.
