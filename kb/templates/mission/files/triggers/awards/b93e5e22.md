# mission awards.js: pattern 2, host-awarded, exactly once (bid, award, take-award); BID_SECONDS 600, CAPACITY 2 and LEASE_SECONDS 300 at the top are the knobs; lands at .knomit/triggers/awards.js

File: `.knomit/templates/mission/.knomit/triggers/awards.js`. Lands at `.knomit/triggers/awards.js` in the new repo.

The trigger script of pattern 2, host-awarded, run by three triggers of the ontology:
- `bid` (on learn of `offers/**`): a machine with capacity writes `bids/<awarder>/<task-id>/<agent-id>/`.
- `award` (on learn of `bids/{agent}/**`, so on the awarder only): the atomic move "write `awards/<winner>/`, delete the offer". A second award for the same offer finds the offer gone and is refused as a whole, so each offer is awarded once.
- `take-award` (on learn of `awards/{agent}/**`, on the winner only): moves the award into `inbox/<agent-id>/working/` with a lease and deletes its bid, in one commit. It takes the award even over capacity.

A losing bid is withdrawn at its `expires` by the inline `withdraw-bid` trigger in the ontology.

**Knobs** (at the top of the file):
- `BID_SECONDS = 600`: how long a bid waits for an award.
- `CAPACITY = 2`: the backlog above which this machine stops bidding.
- `LEASE_SECONDS = 300`: the lease written on a working copy.
