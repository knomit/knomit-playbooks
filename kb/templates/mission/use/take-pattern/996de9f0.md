# Mission edit, the take pattern: the template ships claim / wait / take (tasks, claims) and host-awarded (offers, bids, awards); keep the topics of the one you use and delete the other's

The template ships both ways to take generally available work; no knomit code picks one.

- **Claim / wait / take** (`claims.js`): the topics `tasks` and `claims`. No awarder needed; a double take is possible and is undone by `dup-check`.
- **Host-awarded** (`awards.js`): the topics `offers`, `bids` and `awards`. Exactly one award per offer; needs the awarder (normally the hosting instance) up.

Keep the topics and the script of the one you use, and delete the other's from `.knomit/ontology.yaml` and `.knomit/triggers/`. Keep `inbox`, `acks` and `annotations` either way. A task assigned to one agent goes straight into its `inbox/<agent-id>/working/` under both. README section "Two ways to take a task".
