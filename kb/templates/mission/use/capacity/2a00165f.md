# Mission edit, capacity and lease: CAPACITY in claims.js and awards.js is the per-machine backlog; LEASE_SECONDS is the working copy's lease; the recipe's concurrent is the parallelism

Edit these together:

- `CAPACITY` (2) in `.knomit/triggers/claims.js` and `.knomit/triggers/awards.js`: how many copies, `working/` and `active/` together, a machine holds before it stops claiming and bidding. Keep the two equal.
- `LEASE_SECONDS` (300) in both scripts: the lease on a working copy. When a copy is still in the queue at its lease, the `lease` trigger starts a session for it. Shorter picks up a dropped copy sooner; longer logs fewer `busy` rows while a session runs.
- `concurrent` in the header of `.knomit/recipes/work-task.js` (1): how many sessions run at once on a machine. Raising it above 1 is safe: the take's move keeps sessions off each other's copies.
- `timeout_ms` (and `TIMEOUT_MS`) in the recipe: one session drains the whole queue, so keep it above the time a full backlog takes.
